Privacy Policy for NotchGate

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

Introduction

NotchGate ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our macOS application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

NotchGate is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not:

  • Collect any personal information
  • Track your usage or behavior
  • Transmit your data to external servers
  • Use analytics or advertising SDKs
  • Access your data beyond what's necessary for the app to function

Data Processing

All data processing happens locally on your device:

  • Calendar events are read from your Mac's Calendar app and displayed in the notch. No event data is transmitted or stored externally.
  • Music player information (song title, artist, album) is read directly from your installed media players (Apple Music, Spotify, etc.). No playback data is transmitted.
  • Location is used only to fetch weather information for your approximate location. No precise coordinates are stored or transmitted.
  • Network data is measured locally to display upload/download speeds. No network traffic is monitored or transmitted.
  • Screen captures are only taken when you explicitly trigger a screenshot from the notch. Only the notch area is captured.

Third-Party Services

NotchGate does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. The app accesses:

  • Your installed media players (Music, Spotify, etc.) directly
  • Your Mac's Calendar app via EventKit
  • Your Mac's weather data via local APIs
  • Your local network for speed monitoring

All data remains on your device and is never shared with us or any third party.

Children's Privacy

NotchGate is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy
